## 1. Reframe the expectation (0–12s)

Most useful things I build with AI aren't even apps.

Everyone thinks building with AI means launching a startup, building a SaaS tool, becoming a tech founder, when it actually doesn't have to be that complicated.

The things that have made the biggest difference in my life are way smaller.

## 2. Three things (12–33s)

There are three things that I actually use.

**One:** a workflow I built to pump out digital product pages. It creates the landing page, builds the checkout flow, and the emails that deliver the product to someone who buys it.

What used to take a designer, developer, copywriter, the whole team, just took me and Cursor and a couple of hours. Now I can spin up a page really quickly because I built the system.

## 3. Brand deal pipeline (33–55s)

**Number two:** my brand deal pipeline.

Outreach is not my favorite thing because it's tedious. So before I pitch a brand, Cursor does research for me: what kind of ads are they running? What kind of products are they launching? Are they working with creators?

It takes all that information and then writes an email pitch. Way warmer than saying, hey, do you want to collab?

Overall my outreach got way better because I'm not starting from zero every single time.

## 4. Content bank (55–68s)

**Number three:** my content bank.

It pulls content relevant to me, tracks things that massively overperform, and brings in relevant news and trends that pertain to the world in which I'm creating content in.

Then it organizes everything into ideas, patterns, and angles.

## 5. Close (68–77s)

I didn't learn how to code to build any of this. I just got really clear on what problems I was trying to solve in my own world. And honestly, that's the best skill.
