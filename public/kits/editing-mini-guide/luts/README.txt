Kyndall's LUT pack

My go-to LUT in the course:
S-LOG_3_to_Rec709_v2.cube at 30% intensity.

The other 15 Cinematic V1 files are looks made for LOG footage. Try them on
your own clip and adjust the intensity based on the footage and lighting.

To add one in CapCut:
1. Select your video clip.
2. Open Video > Adjust > Basic and turn on LUT.
3. Open the Name menu, choose Import, and select the .cube file.
4. Adjust the Intensity slider while watching the preview.
