## what i actually did

I wanted to see what I could actually do with AI UGC. So I thought, what if I just make an entire fake brand?

I made a matcha brand. Gave it a product, a whole entire identity, and then I started making ads.

400 of them. In less than an hour. For a brand that does not exist.

The ads came out pretty good. Completely different creators, different concepts, different hooks, different styles. They look like ads you'd potentially see in the wild.

Now I have a full UGC campaign I can use as a spec project. I can show brands: this is what I can make for you.

I made the whole thing with [Arcads](https://arcads.ai/?via=kyndall).

## the process

**1. make a brand that looks real**

Even a fake one. Mine is [anyway](/anyway): a cold matcha latte in a clear slim can. Fridge-cigarette energy. The drink you grab without thinking, not a wellness ritual.

Arcads can scrape a site for brand, product, and photos. So I built a real product page before I generated anything. If you already have a site, use that. If you don't, make one page that looks like the brand exists.

**2. open arcads**

The button on this page is my link. That's the tool.

**3. paste the site, then the brief**

Give it the URL first. Then paste a short brief: what the brand is, who it's for, three features. Don't pretty it up. The copy I used for anyway is below. Steal it and swap in your product.

**4. logo first, then product photos**

Upload the actual logo file, not a photo of the logo sitting on the product. Image models warp letters. Then product: packshot, in-context, lifestyle.

For anyway that order was: stacked red wordmark on transparent, then the can on white, then the can in a bodega cooler, then a flash hero, then a haul, then a street photo.

**5. make a lot of ads**

Different creators. Different concepts. Different hooks. Different styles. That's the point. One ad is a clip. A few hundred is a campaign you can actually show someone.

## steal this brief

Paste this shape into Arcads. Change the product. Keep it this short.

```
How'd you describe your brand?

anyway is a cold matcha latte in a clear slim can. fridge-cigarette energy. the drink you grab without thinking, not a wellness ritual.

Who is your target audience?

people who treat matcha like diet coke. 20s and 30s, city, corner store and late night, not spa.

What are the main features?

1st: ready-to-drink matcha latte, already cold
2nd: japanese matcha and oat milk
3rd: clear slim can, red type, you can see the green
```

## what it's for

I didn't make this to run ads for a drink that isn't real. I made it so I have a spec project: a full UGC campaign that looks like a real brand hired me.

If you make work for brands, that's the steal. Build the fake one so you can show the real ones what you can do.
