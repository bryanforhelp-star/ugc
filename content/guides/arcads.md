The button on this page is [Arcads](https://arcads.ai/?via=kyndall). That's the tool this is about.

## the order

Do the stills first. Then go into Arcads. Don't start in Arcads with nothing to feed it.

**1. Make characters and lifestyle images** in Cursor and ChatGPT.

**2. Open Arcads.** Three rooms: Studio, Workflows, Agent.

**3. Use Studio** when you want to try a look, a talking actor, a still-to-video.

**4. Use Workflows** when you want volume. Connect the steps. Run it.

**5. Use the Agent.** This was my favorite. You talk to it. It runs the generation.

## 1. characters and lifestyle stills first

I generated the people I wanted, and the in-the-world product shots, before I asked Arcads for video.

Cursor if you already work there. ChatGPT if you don't. Same job: stills of faces and stills of the product in real places.

Drop your product photos in. Ask for characters that match the brand, then lifestyle frames those people could be in.

**Characters**

```
make me stills of people who would actually use this product.
not models. not studio lighting.
phone photo, flash or night, different faces, different ages.
i will use these as characters in arcads.
product photos are attached.
```

**Lifestyle**

```
same people, same product.
lifestyle stills: sipping it, holding it to camera, grabbing it from a fridge, walking with it, on a desk, in a city at night.
it should look like a story, not a catalog.
keep the logo readable. do not redraw the type.
```

Save the stills. Those are your characters and your scenes.

## 2. then arcads

Open Arcads. You'll see **Studio**, **Workflow**, and **Agent** at the top.

Bring the brand in: a site if you have one, the logo file (the actual mark, not a photo of the pack), packshots, and the stills you just made. If the type is sitting on the product, Arcads will warp the letters. Logo file first.

My example brand is [anyway](/anyway). A made-up drink. The site is real enough to scrape.

## studio

Studio is the room where you make one thing at a time.

Talking actors. Video. Image. UGC Studio. You pick a model, drop a script or a still, generate.

Use it to see if a character works. Turn a still into a clip. Try a talking-head. This is where I checked whether the people actually looked like people.

UGC Studio is the image tool trained to look like creator photos, not campaign stills. If a character from Cursor/ChatGPT is close, generate more of that person here, then turn it into video.

## workflows

Workflows is the canvas. Nodes. You connect steps and run the whole thing.

One script, a lot of characters. One still, a lot of clips. You are not clicking generate on every ad by hand.

Use this when a look is working and you want more of it. Build it once. Let it run.

## the agent (this was my favorite)

The Agent is a chat inside Arcads. You tell it what you want. It uses the tools.

I didn't want to sit in Studio clicking every variant. I wanted to describe the ads and get a pile of them. That's the Agent.

Tell it the brand. Drop the stills. Tell it the kind of ads. Let it generate.

```
this brand does not exist. treat it like it does.

here are character stills and lifestyle stills.
make ugc ads that look like you'd see them in a feed.
different creators, different hooks, different settings.
phone camera. not a brand film.
keep the product and the logo consistent with the uploads.
don't invent a new pack.
```

If a batch is off, stay in the same chat. More like this photo. Less studio. Different person. Same product.

## what actually came out

The characters were the tell. Arcads was good at people. Not the waxy AI face you scroll past. Different creators, same product, ads that look native.

That's the pitch. Not "go build a fake brand." The fake brand was how I tested it. The result is what Arcads can make when you give it real-looking stills and actually use the features.

I now have a full set of ads I can show brands. That was a side effect. The thing I was testing was the tool.

## if it looks wrong

| What you're seeing | What I did |
|--------------------|------------|
| Faces look generated | Start from your own character stills. Don't rely on a random library face. |
| Logo is melted | Upload the logo file. Don't let it copy type off a product photo. |
| Every ad is the same person | Change the character still. Or tell the Agent: new creator, same product. |
| It looks like a commercial | Force phone camera, night, flash, not looking at the camera. |
| Pack keeps changing | Fewer refs. Packshot + logo + a couple of lifestyle stills. |
