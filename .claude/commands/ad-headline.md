# Headline Generator

Generate high-converting headlines for: $ARGUMENTS

## THE CORE PRINCIPLE

A headline is the hook phase WEAPONIZED into one sentence.

- Full script = Complete AIP journey with all 5 Copy Blocks layered
- Headline = The ATTENTION phase compressed into 8-15 words maximum

A headline contains 2-3 Copy Blocks in one explosive sentence. No more.

---

## HEADLINE vs. SCRIPT

| Dimension | Headline | Script |
|---|---|---|
| Copy Block density | 2-3 blocks in 8-15 words | All 5 blocks across 120-170 words |
| Job | Stop the scroll. Create curiosity. Get the click. | Build intensity. Handle objections. Drive conversion. |
| Approach | ONE big promise + ONE big curiosity hook | Gradual layering and escalation |

## WINNING COPY BLOCK COMBINATIONS

The most proven headline combinations:

### 1. PAIN + CURIOSITY
"There's one 4-word question you must absolutely never ask a man if you want him to be your boyfriend"

### 2. CURIOSITY + PROMISE
"What if I told you there's a way to have all-day energy without stimulants?"

### 3. PROMISE + PROOF
"7,000 people are already using this to feel amazing all day"

### 4. PAIN + PROMISE
"Stop feeling exhausted - here's why coffee isn't working anymore"

### 5. CURIOSITY alone (if strong enough)
"I didn't know what red meat does to cortisol until I saw this"

### 6. CURIOSITY + PROOF + PROMISE + CONSTRAINT (ultra-compressed)
"The 30-Second Morning Routine Giving 7,000 People All-Day Energy (Without Coffee)"
- CURIOSITY: 30-second routine
- PROOF: 7,000 people
- PROMISE: All-day energy
- CONSTRAINT: Without coffee

## THE HEADLINE CONSTRUCTION PROCESS

### Step 1: Pick Your Hook Type

**WHAT hooks** - Focus on the problem itself
"There's one simple sex mistake you must never make..."
"Did you know Amazon has a $50 million fund they have to pay out every month?"

**WHO hooks** - Focus on authority
"As a dermatologist, I've spent 24 years studying skin..."
"Honey, what happened to NOT getting Botox?"

**HOW hooks** - Focus on the method
"Here are the top three dirty self-defense moves..."

**CONTEXT hooks** - Focus on their situation
"You already know real estate builds wealth, but have you considered short-term rentals?"

### Step 2: Add Core Elements
- **Numbers**: "4-word question", "$50 million fund", "24 years"
- **Unusual details**: "biofilm like Saran Wrap", "cortisol triggers"
- **Simplicity markers**: "takes 10 seconds", "3-word phrase"
- **Authority**: "As a dermatologist", "Navy SEALs"

### Step 3: Apply the Tangible Mechanism (CRITICAL)

This is where good headlines become GREAT headlines.

**Formula:** [Specific Time/Number] + [Common Object/Location] + [Action/System]

- NOT "Discover the secret to weight loss" -> "This 7-Second Morning Salt Flush Melts 1lb Daily"
- NOT "Learn to attract women" -> "The Mirror Message Method That Makes Her Think About You All Day"
- NOT "Improve your trading results" -> "The 10am Price Pattern That Signals Winning Trades"

### Step 4: Calibrate to Believability Scale (6-8 Sweet Spot)

- **1-3**: Too boring. "Eating healthy helps you lose weight."
- **4-5**: Interesting but not compelling. "Lose weight faster with this tip."
- **6-8**: THE SWEET SPOT. "That sounds crazy... but what if it's true?"
- **9-10**: Too unbelievable. "Lose 50lbs overnight!"

## THE 4 DOMINANT HEADLINE PATTERNS

### Pattern 1: Forbidden Knowledge
**Structure:** "There's one [specific thing] you must never [action] if you want [desired outcome]"
**Copy Blocks:** PAIN + CURIOSITY + CONSTRAINT

Examples:
- "There's one 4-word question you must never ask a man if you want him to commit"
- "Never eat broccoli? This gut doctor says this 'healthy' vegetable destroys your brain"

### Pattern 2: Unexpected Insight
**Structure:** "I didn't know [common thing] does [surprising effect] until I saw this"
**Copy Blocks:** CURIOSITY + PAIN (implied)

Examples:
- "I didn't know what red meat does to cortisol until I saw this"
- "I didn't know coffee was blocking fat loss until my doctor showed me this scan"

### Pattern 3: Authority Reveal
**Structure:** "As a [credible authority], I've [timeframe/experience] and discovered [shocking truth]"
**Copy Blocks:** PROOF (authority) + CURIOSITY + PAIN

Examples:
- "As a dermatologist, I've spent 24 years studying skin... women over 45 are developing invisible sludge"
- "As a Navy SEAL instructor, I've trained thousands - here's the one move that saves lives"

### Pattern 4: Tangible Mechanism + Proof
**Structure:** "The [Specific Named Method] that [impressive result] ([proof number])"
**Copy Blocks:** CURIOSITY (mechanism) + PROMISE + PROOF + CONSTRAINT

Examples:
- "The 7-Second Morning Salt Flush Melting 1lb Daily (12,847 People Tried It)"
- "The Coffee Shop Close That Doubled My Sales (Even With Zero Experience)"

## HEADLINE-TO-SCRIPT CONTINUITY

Headlines should set up the script. The headline is the compressed version of the script's attention phase:

**Headline:** "The 4-Word Question You Must Never Ask a Man"

**Script opening (expands the headline):**
"There's one 4-word question you must absolutely never ask a man if you want him to be your boyfriend...
As a woman, it'll probably sound completely innocent to you... but as a guy I can tell you that when we hear this question, it takes all the wind out of our romantic sails..."

When generating headlines, note which ones expand naturally into full scripts.

## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC CALIBRATION

### Facebook/Instagram Ads
- 5-12 words in primary text hook
- Emotional resonance, visual language
- Pattern interrupt for feed scrolling

### YouTube Thumbnails/Titles
- 8-12 words max
- Curiosity gap is KING
- Pair with visual thumbnail concept

### Google Ads
- 30 characters max per headline
- Benefit-forward, high intent
- Include keywords naturally

### Email Subject Lines
- 6-10 words ideal
- Curiosity + personalization
- Avoid spam triggers

### Landing Pages
- Can be longer (up to 20 words)
- PROMISE-heavy
- Pair with subheadline that adds PROOF

### TikTok/Reels Text Overlays
- 5-8 words max
- Must work with visual
- Prohibition and contradiction hooks dominate

## QUALITY CHECKLIST

Before finalizing each headline:
- [ ] Understandable in under 3 seconds?
- [ ] Creates an information gap that demands resolution?
- [ ] Believability hits 6-8?
- [ ] Uses tangible mechanism (not abstract benefit)?
- [ ] Stakes are immediately clear?
- [ ] Violates market expectations?
- [ ] Maximum specificity while remaining believable?
- [ ] Viewer thinks "This is about ME"?

---

## CRITICAL RULES

1. NEVER fabricate statistics or proof. Use placeholders if needed.
2. Headlines must be instantly understandable - zero mental processing required.
3. 2-3 Copy Blocks maximum per headline. More = diluted impact.
4. Sound like a real person, not a marketer.
5. Always provide variety across patterns, hook types, and copy block combinations.
6. For each batch, identify the top 3 strongest headlines and explain why.
