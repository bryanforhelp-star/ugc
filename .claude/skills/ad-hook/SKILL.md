# Hook Engineering System

Engineer high-performance hooks for: $ARGUMENTS

## THE HOOK'S SINGULAR PURPOSE

A hook has ONE job: Create enough psychological tension in 3-5 seconds that NOT clicking feels worse than clicking.

Not to educate. Not to be clever. Not to build brand. Stop the scroll. Create irresistible curiosity. Force the click.

---

## BEFORE WRITING: Strategic Setup

### 1. Identify Market Direction
- **TOWARDS** (seeking gain): Lead with PROMISE + CURIOSITY
- **AWAY** (avoiding pain): Lead with PAIN + CURIOSITY or PAIN + PROOF

### 2. Identify Awareness Level
- **Unaware** -> Heavy PAIN + pattern interrupt
- **Problem Aware** -> PAIN + CURIOSITY
- **Solution Aware** -> CURIOSITY + PROMISE
- **Product Aware** -> PROMISE + PROOF

### 3. Run the Curiosity Quadrant
- Market's view of the PROBLEM
- Market's view of the SOLUTION
- Your offer's view of the PROBLEM
- Your offer's view of the SOLUTION

Contradict the market's view on at least one axis to create the curiosity gap.

---

## THE 3 PSYCHOLOGICAL MECHANISMS

Every hook must activate at least one:

### 1. The Information Gap (Loewenstein's Gap Theory)
The brain HATES incomplete information. Create a gap between what they know and what they want to know.
- Reveal that important information EXISTS
- Withhold WHAT that information is
- Make it relevant to their survival/status/success

### 2. Pattern Interruption (Violation of Expectation)
Break the pattern the brain expects to see in this market.
- If everyone says X, you say the opposite of X
- Use contradiction, prohibition, or unexpected results

### 3. Relevance Trigger (Personal Stakes)
Make them feel: "This is about ME. This affects MY life."
- Use "you" language
- Make it personal and immediate
- Create clear stakes

## STEP 1: Choose Your Hook Type

### Type 1: WHAT Hooks (Problem/Discovery Focused)
**Best when:** You have a strong mechanism, hidden cause, or contrarian insight
**Structure:** "There's [specific thing] that [important impact]..."

Examples:
- "There's one simple sex mistake you must never make when being intimate with a man..."
- "Did you know Amazon has a $50 million fund they have to pay out every month?"
- "There's a biofilm forming on your skin right now that's blocking every product you use..."

### Type 2: WHO Hooks (Authority Focused)
**Best when:** You have credible expertise or surprising credentials
**Structure:** "As a [authority], I [discovered/learned] that [surprising truth]..."

Examples:
- "As a dermatologist, I've spent 24 years studying skin at the cellular level and what I found recently changed everything..."
- "As a Navy SEAL instructor, I've trained thousands of soldiers - here's the one move that saves lives..."

### Type 3: HOW Hooks (Method Focused)
**Best when:** You have a unique process, system, or technique
**Structure:** "Here's how to [desired outcome] using [unexpected method]..."

Examples:
- "Here are the top three dirty self-defense moves Navy SEALs actually use..."
- "How to turn $50K into $500K in real estate without touching physical properties..."

### Type 4: CONTEXT Hooks (Situation Focused)
**Best when:** Targeting a specific circumstance or current reality
**Structure:** "You already [current state], but [overlooked opportunity/hidden problem]..."

Examples:
- "You already know real estate builds wealth, but have you considered short-term rentals?"
- "Still scrolling for the fix to your day-trading headaches?"

---

## STEP 2: Apply Core Hook Elements

Every hook needs 3-4 of these:

### Specificity
- Numbers: "4-word question", "$50 million fund", "24 years"
- Unusual details: "biofilm like Saran Wrap", "cortisol triggers"
- Simplicity markers: "takes 10 seconds", "3-word phrase"

### Conflict/Tension
- **Contradiction**: "The best way to make money on Amazon is NOT with physical products"
- **Prohibition**: "There's one thing you must NEVER do..."
- **Hidden Danger**: "This invisible biofilm is blocking everything..."
- **Unexpected Result**: "I worked LESS and made MORE"

### Stakes/Urgency
Make it clear WHY this matters and what happens if they ignore it.
- NOT "Learn about cortisol" -> "What red meat does to cortisol that keeps you fat"

### Curiosity Trigger
- **The Blank Fill**: "Never _____ his _____ with your _____"
- **The Partial Reveal**: "I didn't know what red meat does to cortisol..."
- **The Named Mystery**: "The biofilm problem" / "The rat technique"
- **The Unexpected Source**: "What Navy SEALs know about..." / "The Japanese method for..."

---

## STEP 3: Deploy the Tangible Mechanism Framework

Transform abstract benefits into concrete, visual, memorable concepts.

**Formula:** [Specific Time/Number] + [Common Object/Location] + [Action/System]

| Abstract (Weak) | Tangible Mechanism (Strong) |
|---|---|
| Weight loss supplement | The 7-Second Morning Salt Flush |
| Language learning course | The Movie Mimic Method |
| Sales training | The Coffee Shop Close |
| Trading strategy | The 10am Price Pattern |
| Productivity system | The 5-Minute Filter Screen |
| Dating advice | The Mirror Message Method |

**Why they work:** Visual immediacy, familiar elements, specific process, novel application, memorable, proprietary feel.

**How to create yours:**
1. Identify what the solution actually DOES
2. Find a tangible object, time, or place involved
3. Name it using the formula
4. Test believability (must hit 6-8)

---

## STEP 4: Calibrate to the Believability Scale

- **1-3**: Too boring. "Eating healthy helps you lose weight." Nobody cares.
- **4-5**: Mildly interesting. "Try this new weight loss supplement." Not compelling.
- **6-8**: THE SWEET SPOT. "That sounds crazy... but what if it's true?"
- **9-10**: BS detector triggered. "Lose 50lbs overnight!" Instant distrust.

**How to hit 6-8:**
- Add specificity to increase believability
- Add constraints to increase believability ("after 90 days of consistent work")
- Use ranges instead of absolutes ("20-50 pounds" not "exactly 47 pounds")
- Include realistic timeframes ("7-14 days" not "overnight")

## STEP 5: Add Power Amplifiers

### Emotional Words
| Neutral | Emotionally Charged |
|---|---|
| Important | Critical, Vital, Life-changing |
| Bad | Toxic, Devastating, Sabotaging |
| Good | Transformative, Breakthrough |
| Mistake | Deadly mistake, Fatal flaw |
| Secret | Forbidden secret, Hidden truth |

### Scarcity/Exclusivity Language
"Most people don't know..." / "Only 3 out of 100 get this right..." / "The thing nobody tells you..."

### Prohibition Language
"Never eat broccoli..." / "Stop doing cardio..." / "Don't say this word..."

### Question Formats
Turn statements into questions to increase engagement.

### Timeframe Specificity
"In 24 hours..." / "Before your next date..." / "Every 87 seconds..."

## ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

### The Nested Loop Hook
Open multiple curiosity loops in one hook:
"There's one 4-word question [LOOP 1] you must never ask a man if you want him to commit [LOOP 2] - but 97% of women ask it without knowing [LOOP 3]"

### The Contrast Stack
Stack contrasts to amplify tension:
"Most products cost $200 and barely work. This $47 solution works better. The weird part - dermatologists use it but never recommend it."

### The Authority Flip
Use authority to say the OPPOSITE of what they'd expect:
"As a dermatologist, I tell my patients to avoid sunscreen..."

### The Enemy Naming
Give the problem a memorable name:
- NOT "your metabolism is slow" -> "The cortisol trap keeping you in fat-storage mode"
- NOT "dating is hard" -> "The 3-date ghost pattern ruining your love life"

### The Stat Shock
Use surprising statistics: "House fires occur every 87 seconds in America"
Must be: Surprising, Specific (not rounded), Relevant, Credible.

### The Time Collapse
Create urgency through time compression:
"In the next 24 hours, you'll either know how to fix this or waste another week..."

### The Transformation Tease
Open with the END of a transformation story:
"At age 61, Dr. Sebring was outperforming martial artists half his age..."

## THE HOOK TESTING MATRIX

Before finalizing ANY hook, verify YES to 8/9:

1. Does it stop scrolling in 3 seconds?
2. Does it create an irresistible curiosity gap?
3. Is it calibrated to 6-8 on believability?
4. Does it use a tangible mechanism (not abstract benefit)?
5. Are the stakes immediately clear?
6. Does it violate market expectations (pattern interrupt)?
7. Is it as specific as possible while remaining believable?
8. Does it make the viewer think "This is about ME"?
9. Would I personally click this if I saw it?

## COMMON FAILURES TO AVOID

- **Too Vague**: "Learn the secret to success" -> Add specificity
- **Too Unbelievable**: "Make $100K overnight" -> Add constraints and realistic timeframes
- **Feature-Focused**: "Our AI uses machine learning" -> Translate to human outcome
- **No Stakes**: "Discover this fact about cortisol" -> Add personal consequence
- **No Curiosity Gap**: "Eat vegetables to lose weight" -> Add contradiction or hidden cause
- **Identity Attack**: "Are you a struggling entrepreneur?" -> Reframe as discovery/opportunity

---

## CRITICAL RULES

1. NEVER fabricate statistics or proof. Use [INSERT REAL STAT] placeholders if needed.
2. Each hook must be understandable in 3 seconds or less.
3. Hooks must sound like real human speech, not copywriter jargon.
4. A hook's job is NOT to sell - it's to create enough tension that clicking is the only relief.
5. Always provide variety: mix hook types, formulas, and psychological mechanisms.
