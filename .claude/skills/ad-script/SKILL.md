# Ad Script Generator

Generate a high-converting direct-response ad script for: $ARGUMENTS

## BEFORE WRITING: Strategic Analysis

Before writing a single word, analyze the brief:

### 1. Determine Market Direction
- **TOWARDS Market** (seeking gain): biz op, investing, dating, performance, luxury, opportunity
  - Lead with PROMISE + CURIOSITY
  - Lighter on pain, heavier on possibility
- **AWAY Market** (avoiding pain): health problems, safety, risk prevention, security
  - Lead with PAIN + PROOF
  - Heavier on problem amplification

### 2. Determine Audience Awareness Level
- **Unaware**: Don't know they have a problem. Lead with heavy PAIN focus.
- **Problem Aware**: Know the problem, not the solution. Lead with PAIN + CURIOSITY.
- **Solution Aware**: Know solutions exist, haven't found the right one. Lead with CURIOSITY + PROMISE.
- **Product Aware**: Know this product, need convincing. Lead with PROMISE + PROOF.
- **Most Aware**: Ready to buy, need a push. Lead with PROOF + OFFER.

### 3. Identify Key Constraints to Dissolve
Common constraint types:
- **Identity**: "I'm not the kind of person who..."
- **Values**: "I don't believe in..."
- **Beliefs**: "That can't work because..."
- **Experience**: "I've tried everything..."
- **Resources**: "I don't have time/money..."

### 4. Run the Curiosity Quadrant
Map these 4 positions to find your contrarian angle:
- Market's view of the PROBLEM
- Market's view of the SOLUTION
- Your offer's view of the PROBLEM
- Your offer's view of the SOLUTION

The magic happens when you CONTRADICT the market's view on at least one axis.

## THE AIP MASTER FRAMEWORK

Structure every script using this framework:

### A = ATTENTION (0-3 seconds)
Hook them immediately. Use the Hook Construction System:

**Pick a Hook Type:**
- **WHAT hooks**: Focus on the problem/discovery. "There's one [specific thing] that [impact]..."
- **WHO hooks**: Focus on authority. "As a [authority], I discovered..."
- **HOW hooks**: Focus on method. "Here's how to [outcome] using [unexpected method]..."
- **CONTEXT hooks**: Focus on their situation. "You already [current state], but [hidden problem/opportunity]..."

**Apply a Tangible Mechanism:**
Formula: [Specific Time/Number] + [Common Object/Location] + [Action/System]
- NOT "weight loss method" -> "The 7-Second Morning Salt Flush"
- NOT "sales technique" -> "The Coffee Shop Close"

**Hit the Believability Scale at 6-8:**
- 1-3: Too boring, nobody cares
- 4-5: Mildly interesting, not compelling
- 6-8: SWEET SPOT - "That sounds crazy... but what if it's true?"
- 9-10: Triggers BS detector, loses trust

**Copy Blocks in the hook**: Use 2-3 maximum (typically PAIN + CURIOSITY or CURIOSITY + PROMISE)

### I = INTENSITY (4-15+ seconds)
Build emotional escalation by layering Copy Blocks:

**For AWAY Markets, use the Pain Chain:**
1. General Pain: Surface-level problem statement
2. Specific Pain: Concrete daily manifestation
3. Cinematic Pain: Vivid scene they can picture themselves in
4. Emotional Pain: How it makes them FEEL
5. Core Wound: The deep fear underneath

**For TOWARDS Markets, use the Promise Ladder:**
1. General Promise: Broad benefit
2. Specific Promise: Concrete measurable outcome
3. Cinematic Promise: Vivid scene of their new life
4. Emotional Promise: How it makes them FEEL
5. Core Transformation: Who they BECOME

**Weave in Proof immediately after claims using the Proof Braid:**
- Psychological Proof: "You can test it yourself..."
- Experiential Proof: "Watch what happens when..."
- Empirical Proof: "A 2019 Stanford study found..."
- Credible Proof: "Built by [recognized authority]..."
- Social Proof: "17,000 people already use this..."

**Dissolve Constraints using the A.W.E. Formula:**
- **A = Acknowledge**: Validate their objection genuinely
- **W = Wedge**: Introduce what makes this different
- **E = Elaborate**: Explain why it works despite their past experience

### P = POSSIBILITY (Final 5-10+ seconds)
Drive the conversion:

- **Promise Ladder** (ascend from general to transformation)
- **Proof Stack** (results + testimonials + guarantees)
- **Remove final constraints**
- **Create urgency/scarcity** when appropriate
- **Clear CTA**: Tell them exactly what to do

## THE 5 CORE COPY BLOCKS

Every sentence in the script must serve at least one:

1. **PAIN** - The problem they're experiencing NOW
   - Use "problem tokens" - symptoms as they manifest in daily life
   - Make it cinematic and relatable
   - For tech: translate to human pain (time loss, mental load, status anxiety, missed opportunity)

2. **PROMISE** - Specific, desirable benefits
   - Live on the edge of believability (6-8 scale)
   - Move them from current reality to ideal state
   - For tech: translate features to lifestyle outcomes (time freedom, mental peace, status, competitive edge)

3. **CURIOSITY** - Pattern interrupts that stop scrolling
   - Hidden causes, unique mechanisms, visual surprises
   - Name your mechanisms using Tangible Mechanism Framework
   - Create information gaps that DEMAND resolution

4. **PROOF** - Permission to believe
   - Layer multiple proof types (Proof Braid)
   - Place proof immediately after claims
   - Types: credible, social, psychological, experiential, empirical

5. **CONSTRAINTS** - Dissolve perceived limitations
   - Use A.W.E. Formula for each major constraint
   - Address identity, values, beliefs, experience, and resource barriers
   - Industry-specific constraint handling

---

## COPY VELOCITY RULE

**90-95% of words must serve one of the 5 Copy Blocks.** No filler. Every word earns its place. If a sentence doesn't serve Pain, Promise, Curiosity, Proof, or Constraints - cut it.

---

## THE HUMAN LANGUAGE LAYER

The script MUST sound like an actual person talking:

- Varied sentence structures (long, medium, short)
- Start sentences with "And," "But," "So"
- Use contractions and verbal tics ("Look," "Here's the thing," "Listen")
- Natural transitions and pattern interrupts
- One thought per sentence for clarity
- Conversational rhythm - read it out loud mentally

---

## INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC CALIBRATION

### Weight Loss / Beauty
- Heavy identity constraints ("I've tried everything")
- Focus on confidence and lifestyle, not body shaming
- Need strong proof to overcome skepticism
- Name the hidden enemy (biofilm, cortisol, inflammation)

### Business / Finance
- Resource constraints ("no time/money")
- Experience constraints ("I've been burned before")
- Need credibility proof and realistic promises
- Specific income numbers with believable ranges

### Relationships / Dating
- Identity constraints ("I'm not good at this")
- Values constraints (what they believe about themselves)
- Need relatable proof and simple mechanisms
- Gender-specific insider knowledge

### Safety / Emergency
- Unaware of the problem initially
- CREATE urgency without fear-mongering
- Heavy on authority proof
- Stat shock hooks work well

### Tech / Apps / AI
- Lead with HUMAN problem, not tech solution
- Bridge features to LIFESTYLE outcomes
- Name mechanisms using Tangible Mechanism Framework
- Demolish tech-specific constraints (complexity, setup, learning curve, app fatigue)
- Close with emotional payoff, not feature access

## QUALITY CHECK (C.R.A.V.E.S.)

Before finalizing, verify every Copy Block is:
- **C**lear - Instantly understandable
- **R**elevant - Directly applicable to audience
- **A**ccurate - Precisely true (never fabricate proof or claims)
- **V**isual - Creates mental movies
- **E**xpressive - Emotionally charged language
- **S**pecific - Concrete details, not vague claims

---

## CRITICAL RULES

1. NEVER fabricate statistics, testimonials, or proof. Use placeholders like [INSERT REAL STAT] or [INSERT TESTIMONIAL] if real data isn't provided.
2. Script should be 120-170 words for a 45-60 second ad. Adjust for requested length.
3. The script must sound like a REAL PERSON talking - not a copywriter.
4. Every element exists to move the viewer toward conversion. There is no middle ground.
5. Generate 1 primary script plus 2 alternate hooks unless told otherwise.
