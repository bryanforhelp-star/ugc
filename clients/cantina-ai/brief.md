# Cantina AI — Client Brief

## Product
Cantina AI is an AI "slop" character generator. Users create weird, uncanny AI avatars/characters that can send iMessages and have conversations. Think absurd animated characters delivering unhinged dialogue.

## Content Goal
Organic TikTok / Reels growth. 14 videos per week minimum.

## Format (how every video works)
1. Creator films themselves with a surprised/shocked reaction face
2. On-screen text overlay sets up the scenario
3. iMessage reveal — shows conversation with Cantina bot
4. The Cantina bot dialogue is the punchline (absurd, unhinged, or unexpectedly wholesome)

## Creator
UGC creator filming themselves. No pet. Energy: surprised face → deadpan/unbothered delivery.

## Voice & Tone
- Organic, not ad-like
- Dark humor, relatable chaos, occasional wholesome pivot
- Short and punchy — 15–30 seconds
- Do NOT say "Only Fans" (use OF or abbreviate)
- Do NOT say "Sugar Daddy" (use 🍬ddy or "older guy")

## Payout Requirement
Must complete at least 4 formats per cycle before next payout.
