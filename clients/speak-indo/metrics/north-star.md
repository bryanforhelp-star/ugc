# North-Star Metrics

If only one number mattered: **free signups per day.** That's the daily
heartbeat. At 20–50/day consistent, the subscription business becomes real.

## North star

**Free signups per day.**
- Baseline target by end of month 1: **20/day**
- Stretch target by end of month 1: **50/day**

## Supporting metrics (the funnel)

| Stage | Metric | Target by end of month 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Short-form views (rolling 7d) | 250k+ |
| Click | Profile clicks → site clicks | 3%+ of views |
| Activation | Free signups / day | 20–50 |
| Conversion | Free → Pro % (first 7 days) | 5%+ |
| Plan upgrade | Pro → yearly / lifetime % | 15%+ of paid |
| Retention | Day-7 retention on free | 40%+ |
| Retention | Day-30 retention on paid | 70%+ |
| Paid efficiency | Cost per free signup (paid ads) | < $1.50 |
| Paid efficiency | Cost per paid subscriber (paid ads) | < $25 |

## Per-post log (short-form)

Update daily in a spreadsheet (or this file). One row per post.

| Date | Platform | Script | Archetype | Hook pattern | Views (24h) | Views (7d) | 3s retention % | Profile clicks | Link clicks | Free signups attributed | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

After 14 posts (end of week 1), aggregate by hook pattern:

| Hook pattern | Posts | Avg views | Avg 3s retention | Avg signups |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Top 3 patterns by signups → 70% of week 2 scripts remix those.

## Weekly review (Fridays, 30 min)

Fill in one row per week:

| Week | Free signups | Pro conversions | Top hook | Top SEO page | Partner wins | Cost / free signup | Cost / paid sub | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Three questions to answer every Friday:

1. What hook / page / partner type drove the most signups this week?
2. What's the cheapest way to get the next 100 free signups?
3. What do we *stop* doing next week?

## Monthly review (last day of month)

- CAC by channel (organic short-form, SEO, paid, partnership)
- LTV estimate (avg subscription length × monthly price)
- Churn % (paid month-over-month)
- Top 10 videos all-time
- Top 5 SEO pages by signup
- Best-performing creator archetype
- Best-performing CTA
- Plan next month's campaign hook
