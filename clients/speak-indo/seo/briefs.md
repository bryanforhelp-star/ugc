# SEO Briefs

Three pages to ship in week 1, then 3/week after that. Every page follows the
same structure and ends with the same CTA.

## Universal page structure

1. **H1** — exact-match headline of the search query
2. **2-sentence intro** — name the audience (Bali tourist / expat /
   nomad), name the outcome ("speak enough Bahasa to feel confident in real
   situations"), then drop into the value
3. **Phrase table** — Indonesian / English / when to use it
4. **3–5 mini-scenarios** — short dialogue snippets the reader can copy
5. **Pronunciation tips** — 3–5 bullets
6. **Polite vs. casual register notes** (where relevant)
7. **CTA block** — *"Practice these phrases with speaking drills inside
   Speak Indo. Try it free at [speak-indo.com](https://speak-indo.com)."*
8. **Internal links** — 2–3 to other SEO pages, 1 to the 7-day challenge
9. **FAQ schema block** — 4–6 Q&As

## Universal SEO hygiene

- Title tag: ≤ 60 chars, lead with the keyword
- Meta description: 150–160 chars, end with *"Practice free at speak-indo.com."*
- URL slug: kebab-case, keyword-only (`/indonesian-phrases-for-bali`)
- Image alt text on every image
- Schema: Article + FAQPage
- Word count target: 1,000–1,400
- Reading level: 6th–8th grade

## Brief 1 — *Indonesian phrases for Bali*

- **Target query:** "indonesian phrases for bali"
- **Slug:** `/indonesian-phrases-for-bali`
- **Search intent:** trip-prep, tourist, expat — wants a usable list before
  arrival
- **Estimated difficulty:** medium-low — long-tail with high intent
- **Title tag:** *Indonesian Phrases for Bali: 25 Words You'll Actually Use*
- **Meta description:** *The practical Bahasa starter pack for Bali — what
  to say at warungs, with Gojek drivers, and at your villa. Practice free
  at speak-indo.com.*

**Required sections / phrases**
- Greetings: *halo, selamat pagi, selamat malam, terima kasih (kak),
  sama-sama*
- Food: *saya mau, jangan pedas, enak sekali, sudah terima kasih, berapa?*
- Drivers: *halo pak, saya di depan, terima kasih pak*
- Villa: *halo saya check-in, atas nama, di mana kamar?*
- Bargaining: *boleh kurang?, terlalu mahal, oke*
- Help: *pelan-pelan kak, saya tidak mengerti, maaf*

**Mini-scenarios**
1. Ordering at a warung
2. Answering a Gojek call
3. Checking into a villa
4. Asking the price at a market

**Internal links**
- → *How to order food in Indonesian* (Brief 2)
- → *Indonesian phrases for Gojek and Grab* (Brief 3)
- → 7-day Bali Bahasa Challenge

**Primary CTA placement:** after the phrase table, again at the bottom.

## Brief 2 — *How to order food in Indonesian*

- **Target query:** "how to order food in indonesian" / "ordering food in
  bahasa"
- **Slug:** `/how-to-order-food-in-indonesian`
- **Search intent:** Bali tourists who want to confidently order at warungs
- **Estimated difficulty:** low — mostly informational
- **Title tag:** *How to Order Food in Indonesian (Bali Warung Edition)*
- **Meta description:** *Order nasi goreng like you live there. Real
  phrases for warungs, cafes, and street food in Bali. Practice free at
  speak-indo.com.*

**Required sections**
1. The full ordering sentence: *Saya mau [dish], jangan pedas, satu.*
2. Building blocks table: *saya mau / jangan / pedas / satu, dua, tiga /
   bungkus / minum / tanpa*
3. Top 10 dishes you'll order in Bali (nasi goreng, mie goreng, nasi
   campur, gado-gado, sate ayam, etc.) with pronunciation
4. Drinks: *es teh, kopi susu, air putih, jus jeruk*
5. *To go vs. dine in:* *bungkus* vs *makan di sini*
6. Polite phrases: *enak sekali, sudah terima kasih, boleh minta…*
7. What to say if it's wrong: *maaf, saya pesan… (no spice / not this)*

**Mini-scenarios**
1. First time at a warung
2. Picking from a Padang display
3. Ordering coffee
4. Asking for takeaway

**Internal links**
- → *Indonesian phrases for Bali* (Brief 1)
- → *How to say "no spice" in Indonesian* (week 2 brief)
- → 7-day Bali Bahasa Challenge

**Primary CTA placement:** mid-article (after the *Saya mau* block) and at
the end.

## Brief 3 — *Indonesian phrases for Gojek and Grab*

- **Target query:** "indonesian phrases for gojek" / "what to say to gojek
  driver"
- **Slug:** `/indonesian-phrases-for-gojek`
- **Search intent:** very high-intent — already in Bali, app open, driver
  calling
- **Estimated difficulty:** low — niche, almost no one is writing this
- **Title tag:** *What to Say to a Gojek Driver: Indonesian Phrases for
  Bali*
- **Meta description:** *Your Gojek driver is calling and you can't speak
  Indonesian. Here's exactly what to say — every phrase you'll need.
  Practice free at speak-indo.com.*

**Required sections**
1. The single phrase that solves 80% of calls: *halo, pak — saya di depan*
2. Phrase table: *saya di depan, saya di lobi, saya pakai baju [color],
   tunggu sebentar, satu menit, di mana?, sudah sampai*
3. Pickup-side: how to confirm the location, what to say if you're moving
4. Drop-off side: *di sini saja, terima kasih pak*
5. Tipping & politeness: short note on *pak / bu* and adding *terima
   kasih*
6. What to do if the driver speaks too fast: *pelan-pelan, kak*
7. Common food-delivery callouts (GoFood / GrabFood): *taruh di depan
   pintu*

**Mini-scenarios**
1. Driver calls — ride pickup
2. Driver calls — food delivery, dropoff instructions
3. You're running late: *tunggu sebentar*
4. They drove past you: *kembali, kak — saya di belakang*

**Internal links**
- → *Indonesian phrases for Bali* (Brief 1)
- → *How to say "I'm out front" in Indonesian* (a follow-up sub-brief)
- → 7-day Bali Bahasa Challenge

**Primary CTA placement:** right after the *saya di depan* block (people
who just need that phrase will bounce — give them the CTA fast), and at the
end.
