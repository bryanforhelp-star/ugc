# Speak Indo — ASAP GTM Action Plan

> Generated externally on 2026-04-27. Captures the audience map, channel strategy,
> content angles, and 30-day sprint that the scripts in this folder are built against.
> If you're writing new scripts, read `scripting-style.md` first, then use this as
> source-of-truth for which angle / audience to target.

## Do This First

- **Primary bet:** target people already in Indonesia or about to go, especially Bali. Do not start with generic "language learners."
- **Fastest message:** "Stop freezing at warungs, Grab rides, hotels, and small talk. Learn useful Indonesian in 5 minutes/day."
- **Fastest offer:** free 7-day "Survival Indonesian for Bali" challenge, powered by the existing free tier.
- **Fastest channels:** TikTok/Reels organic, Bali Facebook groups, Reddit/community feedback posts, micro-creators, coworking/hostel/surf-camp partnerships, and a small Google Search test.
- **First paid test:** Google Search, not TikTok. Cleanest intent signal. Tiny budget until signup and activation tracking is trustworthy.
- **Ignore for now:** product directories, big SEO projects, broad awareness ads, polished brand campaigns, generic "learn Indonesian" thought leadership.

## Key Survival Phrases (use across content)

- *Boleh minta menu?* — May I have the menu?
- *Tidak pedas ya.* — Not spicy, please.
- *Berapa harganya?* — How much is it?
- *Bisa lebih murah?* — Can it be cheaper?
- *Di mana toilet?* — Where is the toilet?
- *Tolong bicara pelan-pelan.* — Please speak slowly.
- *Saya tidak mengerti.* — I don't understand.
- *Bisa pakai bahasa Inggris?* — Can we use English?
- *Kiri / kanan / lurus / berhenti.* — Left / right / straight / stop.
- *Saya mau pesan ini.* — I want to order this.
- *Saya sedang belajar bahasa Indonesia.* — I'm learning Indonesian.
- *Sudah makan?* — Have you eaten? (cultural how-are-you)

| 5 | "I live in Bali but panic speaking" + IG poll | Follow up creators / coworking | Email capture / phrase lead magnet |
| 6 | "7-day challenge day 1" post | Ask 5 early users for quotes | Add real social proof |
| 7 | Recap: "What Bali learners struggled with most" | Best 5 partners from replies | Decide keep/kill on Google keywords + top angle |

**Daily minimums:** 3 short videos · 1 community/value post · 20 outreach DMs · reply to every comment with another phrase.

- **User interviews:** 20+

## What To Ignore For 30 Days

- Broad "learn a language" SEO
- Big influencers with vague travel audiences
- Product Hunt / directories
- Paid TikTok cold campaigns
- App-store strategy
- Generic grammar content
- Any channel that can't produce signups or feedback within 7 days
