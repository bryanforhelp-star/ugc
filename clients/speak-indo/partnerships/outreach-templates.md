# Partnership Outreach

Goal: send **20 messages in week 1, 30 in week 2.** Don't ask for anything
big up front — lead with a free, useful thing.

## Target list

In priority order — easiest "yes" at the top.

1. **Bali travel creators on TikTok / IG** — 10k–500k followers, Bali-only
   niche
2. **"Moving to Bali" YouTube creators** — long-form expat advice channels
3. **Bali expat Facebook group admins** — *Bali Expats, Bali Digital
   Nomads, Bali Long-term, Canggu Community*
4. **Coworking spaces** — Dojo, Tropical Nomad, BWork, Outpost, Genesis
5. **Villa managers / agencies** — Villa-Finder, Bali Villa Escapes, etc.
6. **Visa agencies** — KITAS / B211A / second-home visa agents
7. **Bali relocation newsletters** — Bali Bible, *The Yak,* Honeycombers Bali
8. **Indonesian teachers on TikTok** — already producing Bahasa content,
   open to affiliate
9. **Bali travel blogs** — Practical Wanderlust, The Bali Bible, etc.
10. **Digital nomad communities** — Nomad List forums, Slack groups,
    Discord servers

## Offer matrix

Pick the offer based on the partner's leverage.

| Partner type | Offer |
|---|---|
| Small creator (< 50k) | Free lifetime access + 30% affiliate commission + custom code |
| Mid creator (50k–500k) | Affiliate (40% first month, 20% recurring) + custom landing page |
| Large creator (500k+) | Sponsored video + revenue share + custom landing page |
| FB group admin | Free lifetime access + 20% affiliate on group code |
| Coworking / villa | Free starter pack PDF for guests + 30% affiliate |
| Visa / relocation agency | "Bahasa Starter Pack" co-branded PDF + 30% affiliate |
| Newsletter | 30% affiliate on dedicated send + free swap content |

## Tracking

Every message goes in a simple sheet (or Notion DB) with these columns:

| Sent | Channel | Partner | Tier | Template | Reply? | Outcome | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Review every Friday. After 50 sends, look at:

- Reply rate by template
- Reply rate by tier
- Conversion rate from yes → first signup attributed
- Best-performing partner archetype
