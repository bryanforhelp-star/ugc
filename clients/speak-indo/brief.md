# speak-indo.com

## Product

**Speak Indo** — focused web/PWA app for learning Bahasa Indonesia. Indonesian-only (no 40-language template). Installable to home screen on iOS/Android, works offline.

**Tiers:**
- **Free forever** — 10 new words/day, 1 quiz/day, unlimited reviews, full library browsable
- **Pro** — unlimited new words/quizzes, speaking practice (mic-scored), full-sentence typing, analytics. Monthly / yearly / lifetime, priced in EUR + IDR. AI tutor + custom decks coming.

**Learning modes:** flashcards, quizzes, listening, speaking (mic), reading/dialogues, grammar, multiplayer.

**Content depth:** A1 / A2 / B1. Decks include greetings, food, travel, airport, hotel, getting around, culture, survival phrases, emergencies, money, WhatsApp etiquette.

**Existing positioning copy on site:** "Learn Bahasa, fast" · "100% focused on Bahasa Indonesia" · "Order food in week 1" · "Hold a real conversation in a month."

## Audiences (priority order)

1. **Bali/Indonesia residents who still don't speak Bahasa** — embarrassment + daily friction. *"Been in Bali 6 months and still only know makasih?"*
2. **Bali-bound travelers** — survival phrases for warungs, Grab, hotels, prices, directions. *"30 phrases before your trip."*
3. **Digital nomads in Bali** — coworking, café, scooter rental, repeated local friction.
4. **Expats relocating to Indonesia** — first-month friction (food, transport, rent, small talk).
5. **Foreigners dating/married to Indonesians** — family, food, affection, respect. Highest emotional retention. *"Sudah makan? Here's what it means."*

## Core problem & desire

People who need real, daily-useful Indonesian get stuck because:
- Big language apps either don't have Indonesian (Duolingo) or treat it as a generic Romance-template course.
- Tourist phrasebooks teach "the apple is red" — not "tidak pedas."
- Living in Indonesia without Bahasa = constant micro-embarrassment + missing the local layer.

What they want: **to stop freezing**. To order food, take a Grab, talk to their landlord, text their partner's mom — without translating, without panic, without switching to English.

## Voice & visual style

- Casual, self-aware, slightly self-deprecating
- Native to TikTok/Reels — confession, POV, observation, story
- Bahasa words inline (italicized, with quick translation) — never sterile
- Visuals: warungs, Grab cars, Canggu/Ubud streets, scooters, WhatsApp screens, market scenes
- Avoid: tourist-board polish, stock-photo Bali, motivational language-app voiceover

## Channel priority

TikTok organic + Reels first. IG Stories for cross-post. Paid (Google Search small test → Meta Reels) only after 2-3 organic angles prove out.

## Strongest angles (validated by GTM audit)

1. Indonesian-only Duolingo alternative
2. Survival Bahasa before Bali (traveler)
3. Resident embarrassment ("still only saying terima kasih")
4. Dating/family ("sudah makan?")
5. Free forever, 5 min/day

## Conversion notes

- Site CTA opens an account modal with strict password requirements — friction. Google sign-in should dominate.
- No social proof above the fold. Need 3 beta quotes from Bali residents/travelers ASAP.
- Hero "Learn Bahasa, fast" is broad — segmented landing pages (`/bali`, `/dating-indonesian`, `/duolingo-alternative`) would convert better.
