# 30-Day Acquisition Plan

Goal at the end of 30 days: **20–50 free signups/day, consistently**, with a
known cost per paid subscriber and a winning short-form ad creative.

Three channels, in priority order:

1. **Short-form video** (TikTok, IG Reels, YouTube Shorts) — main lever
2. **SEO content** — slow but compounding
3. **Partnerships** — Bali-bound traffic from where the audience already is

---

## Week 1 — Lay the rails, test all three archetypes

**Posture:** ship volume, learn fast, don't optimize yet.

| Lever | Output |
|---|---|
| Short-form | **14 videos** (2/day) — see `scripts/week-1/` |
| Archetype mix | 7 foreign learner POV, 4 Indonesian coach, 3 faceless |
| SEO | **3 pages** published — see `seo/briefs.md` |
| Partnerships | **20 outreach DMs/emails** — see `partnerships/outreach-templates.md` |
| Tracking | Log every post in `metrics/north-star.md` (views, profile clicks, signups) |

**Friday review:** Which 3 hooks got the most views? Which archetype had the
highest profile click-through? Which CTA copy converts better — "practice this
free" vs "start learning free"?

**Definition of done for week 1:**
- [ ] 14 videos posted across TikTok + Reels + Shorts
- [ ] 3 SEO pages live
- [ ] 20 outreach messages sent, replies tracked
- [ ] Top 3 hooks identified
- [ ] Baseline numbers in `metrics/north-star.md`

---

## Week 2 — Double down on what's working

| Lever | Output |
|---|---|
| Short-form | **14 videos** — 70% based on top 3 hook patterns from week 1 |
| New formats | 10 faceless phrase videos, 5 in-app demo recordings |
| SEO | **3 more pages** (next 3 from `seo/briefs.md` backlog) |
| Partnerships | **30 more outreach messages**, prioritizing the response patterns that worked |
| Email | Set up a basic welcome sequence for free signups (3 emails) |

**Friday review:** Free signups/day trend (line should be up). Top archetype
locked in. First partnership confirmed?

---

## Week 3 — Productize a campaign + recruit affiliates

| Lever | Output |
|---|---|
| Campaign | Launch **"7-Day Bali Bahasa Challenge"** — landing page + 7 daily lessons + email sequence |
| Short-form | **14 videos** — one per day teaches the daily challenge phrase |
| Affiliates | Onboard 3–5 creators with custom code + 30% commission |
| Email capture | Lead magnet on every SEO page: *"Free Bali Bahasa Starter Pack (PDF)"* |
| CTA test | A/B "practice this free" vs "start the 7-day challenge free" |

**Friday review:** Challenge signups, email→pro conversion, affiliate-attributed signups.

---

## Week 4 — Turn winners into paid ads

| Lever | Output |
|---|---|
| Paid ads | Re-cut the top 3 organic videos as ads. **$10/day per creative**, Meta + TikTok |
| Landing | Send paid traffic to the highest-converting angle from weeks 1–3 |
| Pricing test | Push **yearly plan** or **founder lifetime offer** on free→pro upsell |
| Short-form | **14 videos** — keep the organic engine running |
| SEO | **3 pages** — fill the long-tail backlog |

**Friday review (the big one):**
- Free signups/day — at or above 20/day baseline?
- Free → pro conversion %
- Cost per free signup (paid)
- Cost per paid subscriber (paid)
- Which channel has the best CAC? Lean in for month 2.

---

## Cadence cheat sheet

**Daily**
- Post 2 short-form videos (one in the morning, one late afternoon Bali time)
- Reply to every comment for the first 2 hours after posting
- Log post stats in `metrics/north-star.md`

**Weekly (Friday, 30 min)**
- Update `metrics/north-star.md` weekly summary row
- Pick the next 14 scripts; greenlight or rewrite based on hook winners
- Send the next batch of partnership outreach
- Publish 3 SEO pages

**Monthly**
- Full review: CAC by channel, churn, top 10 videos, top 5 SEO pages
- Plan next month's campaign hook
