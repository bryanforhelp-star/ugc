# 13 — AI roleplay

I've been using Speak Indo for a week and the feature that's actually changed my life is the AI roleplay.

You pick a scenario — like, "Gojek driver is calling you" — and the AI literally calls you. In Indonesian. With a real-sounding voice. And you have to actually answer or it just waits.

The first time? I froze. Total deer in headlights. The driver goes *"halo, pak — anda di mana?"* and I just sat there.

Second time I got it right. *Saya di depan.* Got a little green checkmark. I felt feral.

Practicing actual conversations is wildly different from flashcards. If you're going to Bali — this is the only thing that's actually prepared me for talking to real people. Speak Indo, link in bio.
