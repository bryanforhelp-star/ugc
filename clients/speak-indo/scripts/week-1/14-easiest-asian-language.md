# 14 — Easiest Asian language

I'll die on this hill — Indonesian is the easiest Asian language to learn. Easier than Mandarin. Japanese. Korean. Honestly easier than Spanish. Three reasons.

One — no tones. Words mean what they mean. You don't have to sing them.

Two — no conjugation. The verb "eat" is *makan* in every situation. Past, future, you, me, them. Same word.

Three — Latin alphabet. No new script. You can read it day one.

Plus: plurals are repeating the word. Gender doesn't exist. The whole grammar was designed by committee in 1928 to be consistent.

I've been on Speak Indo for a week and I can already have basic conversations. If you've been putting off learning a language because you think it'll take three years — try this one.
