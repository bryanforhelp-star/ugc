# 01 — Indonesian has no tenses

I just learned Indonesian has zero tenses and I'm honestly furious.

I've been studying it for Bali on this app, Speak Indo, and the lesson today was — past tense? add the word *kemarin*. Future? add *besok*. That's it. There's no past perfect, no continuous, none of that.

I was over here panicking about conjugations for three weeks and the answer was just… use a different time word.

English speakers got robbed. If you're going to Bali, genuinely just try this app. It's the first time learning a language hasn't made me feel stupid.
