# 07 — Bahasa was invented in 1928

OK Indonesian is wild — the language was basically invented by committee in 1928.

Indonesia has like 700 native languages across thousands of islands. So a group of nationalists got together and were like, "we need one shared language." They picked a version of Malay, cleaned it up, made it consistent, and that's the Bahasa Indonesia people speak today.

Which is why it's so weirdly logical. No tones. No genders. No conjugation. The grammar was literally designed to be learnable.

I've been on Speak Indo for a week and I genuinely understand how Indonesian works — which has never happened to me with any other language. It was built on purpose. We should all be learning this one.
