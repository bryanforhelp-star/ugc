# 04 — The three "no"s

Why do tourists in Bali keep getting spicy food when they ask for no spice?

Found out today — Indonesian has three different words for "no." Three.

*Tidak* is regular no. *Jangan* is "don't," like a command. *Bukan* is "it's not."

I was saying *tidak pedas* — which apparently sounds like "no spicy" as a polite opinion, not a request. The cook nods and keeps cooking. You're supposed to say *jangan pedas.* *Don't* spicy. Stop the spicy from happening.

I learned this on Speak Indo. They had a warung roleplay where I got it wrong and the AI brought me sambal anyway. Mortifying. But also — never making that mistake in real life now.
