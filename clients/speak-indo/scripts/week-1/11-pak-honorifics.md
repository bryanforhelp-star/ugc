# 11 — Why your driver calls you *pak*

If your Gojek driver in Bali calls you *pak* even though you're 23 in a tank top — don't get weird about it.

Indonesian doesn't have one universal "sir" or "ma'am." It has a whole layered honorific system, and once you learn it, everything makes more sense.

*Pak* — older man, or any man you respect.
*Bu* — older woman, same idea.
*Mas* — younger guy, like "bro."
*Mbak* — younger woman, like "miss."
*Kak* — older sibling, gender-neutral, super common.

Drivers default to *pak* and *bu* because it's safer. They're being polite, not condescending.

Speak Indo has a whole module on this and it changed how I read every interaction in Bali. When the driver says *halo, pak* — you say *halo, pak* back. Easy.
