# 09 — Plural = say it twice

Y'all the plural rule in Indonesian is wild. You just say the word twice. That's it.

*Orang* is person. *Orang-orang* is people.
*Anak* is child. *Anak-anak* is children.
*Teman* is friend. *Teman-teman* is friends.

Years of English grammar — irregular plurals, mouse becomes mice, all the exceptions — and I'm just over here doubling words.

I learned this on Speak Indo this morning and I genuinely laughed out loud at how easy it is. Why didn't anyone tell us Indonesian was like this? I keep expecting there to be a catch and there just… isn't.
