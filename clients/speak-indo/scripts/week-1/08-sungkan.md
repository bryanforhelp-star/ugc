# 08 — *Sungkan*

There's a word in Indonesian I think about every day now. *Sungkan*.

It's the awkwardness of asking someone for too much. The discomfort of imposing. The "I don't want to be a bother" feeling — but as a real, named, social emotion that shapes how Indonesians actually behave.

There's no word for this in English. We just impose.

I learned it on Speak Indo's culture section, and now I see it everywhere. My Indonesian friend doesn't ask for the favor she actually needs — *sungkan*. She brings snacks she didn't have to bring — also *sungkan*. The whole social culture is calibrated around it.

Once you know the word, you start feeling it yourself. And you become a slightly better houseguest.
