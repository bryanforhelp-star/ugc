# 10 — *Sudah* past tense hack

Past tense in Indonesian is one word. One.

The word is *sudah* — it means "already." You stick it in front of any verb.

*Saya makan* — I eat.
*Saya sudah makan* — I already ate.

*Saya pergi* — I go.
*Saya sudah pergi* — I already went.

That's the entire past tense. No conjugation. No "eat-ate-eaten." Just *sudah* and the verb you already know.

Speak Indo taught me this in like a 3-minute lesson and I sat there waiting for the rest. There is no rest. That's it.

Bonus — Indonesians ask *"sudah makan?"* as a greeting. "Have you eaten yet?" It's so much more useful than "how are you." Going to Bali a different person.
