# 06 — *Boleh* cheat code

If I had to give one piece of advice to anyone going to Bali — learn the word *boleh*. That's it. One word.

*Boleh* means "can" or "may." You stick it in front of literally anything you want.

*Boleh kurang?* — can it be cheaper.
*Boleh foto?* — can I take a photo.
*Boleh masuk?* — can I come in.
*Boleh tanya?* — can I ask something.

That's the whole tourist experience. One word. Infinite uses. Polite every time.

I learned it on Speak Indo my first day, and I'm telling you — every single interaction in Bali got fifty percent easier. If you only learn one Indonesian word before your trip, make it this one.
