# Week 1 — AI UGC Creator Scripts

14 scripts. Each is the spoken dialogue an AI UGC creator says to camera.
~20–30 seconds each, ~100–130 words.

**Format per script:** single AI character, talking head, captions burnt in,
casual delivery. No b-roll required (optional cutaways for proof).

**Voice rules:**
- First person, personal stake (going to Bali / moving / has a partner / on
  a trip).
- Hook in the first 5 words — specific personal claim or pattern interrupt.
- Speak Indo woven into the *middle* of the script, not slapped on the end.
- Conversational rhythm — run-ons, "ok," "y'all," "I'm not joking," real
  speech patterns.
- Soft endorsement close, never "download now."

**Ship:** 2/day for 7 days.
