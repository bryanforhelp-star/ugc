# 03 — Gojek 6 words

If you're going to Bali, your Gojek driver is going to call you. And he doesn't speak English. Genuinely the scariest part of being a tourist there.

Speak Indo has this scenario where you literally roleplay the call, and they break it down to the six words you actually need.

*Halo. Di mana. Depan. Sampai. Terima kasih. Ya.*

Hello. Where. Front. Arrived. Thank you. Yeah.

He says "where," you say "front." He says "arrived," you say "yeah, thanks." That's the whole call.

I've done this drill like ten times. Going to Bali next month and for the first time I'm not panicking about it.
