# 02 — The *sayang* trap

This is your warning before Bali.

There's an Indonesian word — *sayang*. It means "darling." It also means "what a waste." Same word. No warning label.

I learned this the hard way on Speak Indo. They had this roleplay where I drop something at a warung and go *"sayang!"* meaning *what a shame.* And the AI server paused and goes — *"you just called the cashier honey."*

I almost spit out my drink.

Anyway. Don't do that in Bali. Practice on the app first so the only person you embarrass is the AI.
