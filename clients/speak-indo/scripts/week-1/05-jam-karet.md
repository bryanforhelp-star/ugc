# 05 — *Jam karet*

If you're moving to Bali, please learn this concept now. It's called *jam karet*.

Literally translates to "rubber time." Means: nobody's actually on time. Three o'clock means around three. Maybe four. Possibly Tuesday.

This is not flakiness. It's a real cultural thing. I almost lost my mind when my landlord said he'd come at 2 and showed up at 4:47.

Then I read about *jam karet* in Speak Indo's culture lessons and I was like — oh. We're doing this differently here.

Indonesian doesn't actually have a word for "on time." That tells you everything. Adjust accordingly or you're going to have a 12-month rage stroke.
