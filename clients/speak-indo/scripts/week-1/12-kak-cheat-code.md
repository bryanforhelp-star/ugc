# 12 — *Kak* cheat code

The best Indonesian word I've learned for Bali is the smallest one. It's *kak*.

Literally means "older sibling." But functionally, it's how you call anyone who isn't clearly older or younger than you. It's neutral. It's friendly. It's a one-syllable charm offensive.

Server at a warung — *kak*. Coffee guy — *kak*. Stranger asking directions — *kak*. The person you accidentally bumped on a scooter — *kak*.

*Terima kasih, kak. Permisi, kak. Berapa, kak?*

Locals smile differently when you do this. I'm not joking.

Speak Indo drilled this into me on day one and it's the single most useful word I know. If you only learn one Indonesian word for Bali, learn *kak*.
