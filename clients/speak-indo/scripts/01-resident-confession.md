# Script 01 — Resident Confession

**Angle:** "Been in Bali 6 months and still only know makasih?"
**Audience:** Foreigners living in Bali / Indonesia 3+ months who haven't learned Bahasa
**Format:** Confession / POV, talking head + B-roll cuts
**Length:** ~30 seconds (~115 words)
**Avatar:** 25–35, casual, slight self-deprecation, lived-in look. Café, kitchen, or warung backdrop.

## Strategy

- **Market direction:** AWAY (avoid embarrassment) → TOWARDS payoff (be the foreigner who actually integrates)
- **Awareness:** Problem-aware (knows they should, hasn't)
- **Primary constraint:** IDENTITY — *"I'm not a language person"* + EXPERIENCE — *"I tried, gave up"*
- **Contrarian angle:** Market thinks the answer is Duolingo or classes. Speak Indo's wedge: Indonesian-only, 5 min/day, real-life phrases (warung, Grab, landlord), not textbook ones.
- **Hook type:** Confession + specific number (18 months / two phrases)

## Script

```
00:00–00:03  HOOK
VISUAL: Talking head, café/warung in background, natural light, casual.
LINE: "Confession. I've lived in Bali for eighteen months and the only Indonesian
       I actually speak is terima kasih and tidak pedas. That's the entire list."

00:03–00:11  PAIN STACK (specific, daily, cinematic)
VISUAL: Quick cuts —
        (a) POV warung order, person pointing at menu
        (b) Grab car, driver glancing back
        (c) WhatsApp screen with Indonesian message
LINE: "My Grab driver asks me something and I just smile. My landlord texts me
       in Bahasa and I screenshot it to my friend Sari to translate."

00:11–00:16  CONSTRAINT DISSOLVE (A.W.E.)
VISUAL: Back to talking head, leaning in.
LINE: "And I tried Duolingo. Indonesian's not even on there."

00:16–00:24  BRAND MENTION (single, casual, story-embedded)
VISUAL: Phone in hand, Speak Indo app open, swiping through food/warung deck.
LINE: "Someone tagged me in Speak Indo last week. It's just Indonesian.
       First sentence it taught me — tolong bicara pelan-pelan.
       Please speak slowly. Genuinely the most useful sentence I've ever learned."

00:24–00:30  PAYOFF / SOFT CLOSE (no CTA)
VISUAL: Avatar at café counter, ordering coffee in Bahasa, barista smiles back.
LINE: "Twelve days in. Yesterday I ordered coffee in Bahasa and the barista
       smiled at me like a person, not a tourist. So we're getting somewhere."
```

## Alternate hooks (test these against the primary)

1. **POV variant:** "POV: you've been in Bali so long you have a regular warung — and you still order in English."
2. **Self-roast:** "Plot twist: I've lived in Bali two years and I still freeze every time someone says something to me in Bahasa."
3. **Stat-shock:** "Indonesian has 270 million speakers. Duolingo doesn't have it. Make that make sense."

## Seedance / HeyGen production direction

- **HeyGen avatar:** Female or male, 25–35, casual t-shirt, Bali-coded (not styled). Warm key light, natural shadows. Two angles — locked talking head + slight handheld variant.
- **Seedance B-roll prompts** (save to `prompts/`):
  - `"warung interior, person from behind ordering food, pointing at handwritten menu, golden hour, shallow depth, handheld"`
  - `"Grab motorbike rear view, Bali traffic, driver glancing back, monsoon afternoon light"`
  - `"close-up phone screen showing WhatsApp message in Indonesian, hand visible, café table"`
  - `"Bali café counter, barista smiling, customer paying, soft morning light"`

## Copy block velocity

- Pain ~30% (warung, Grab, landlord beats)
- Constraint ~10% (Duolingo dismissal)
- Curiosity ~20% (mechanism: Indonesian-only)
- Promise ~15% (the barista beat)
- Connective/native rhythm ~25% (the cost of sounding human; lower velocity than a paid ad on purpose)

**Believability rating:** 7.5/10 — specific (18 months, 12 days, friend named Sari, exact phrase taught), no claims that strain credibility.
