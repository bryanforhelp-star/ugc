# Script 02 — Bali Trip Story

**Angle:** "Things I learned the hard way before going back to Bali"
**Audience:** Travelers planning a Bali trip, English speakers 25–45, especially returning visitors or first-timers researching
**Format:** Story-led, advice-from-experience
**Length:** ~40 seconds (~140 words)
**Avatar:** 25–40, at home/desk/airport, casual, tells the story like a friend giving real advice.

## Strategy

- **Market direction:** TOWARDS (better trip, deeper experience) with mild AWAY undercurrent (don't be the clueless tourist)
- **Awareness:** Solution-aware (tried Duolingo or assumed they don't need anything)
- **Primary constraint:** BELIEF — *"Everyone speaks English in Bali"* + EXPERIENCE — *"I tried Duolingo, gave up"*
- **Contrarian angle:** Market view: "you don't need Indonesian for Bali." Wedge: "with 30 phrases your trip is a different country — better food, better prices, better stories."
- **Hook type:** Confession + setup ("number one is the most embarrassing")

## Script

```
00:00–00:04  HOOK
VISUAL: Avatar at desk/window, casual, slight smile, holding nothing.
LINE: "Things I wish someone told me before my first Bali trip — and number one
       is the most embarrassing one."

00:04–00:14  PAIN — STORY BEAT
VISUAL: Photos / B-roll from the "first trip" — generic Canggu, then Sidemen
        (rural village), confused face at warung.
LINE: "I went for two weeks. Assumed everyone spoke English. They mostly do
       in tourist spots. The second I left Canggu and went to Sidemen,
       I was useless. Couldn't order food. Couldn't ask for the bill.
       Couldn't tell my driver where to stop."

00:14–00:24  CINEMATIC PROMISE / CULTURAL INSIGHT
VISUAL: B-roll — warung locals smiling, market scene, sambal being made,
        scooter ride at sunset.
LINE: "And here's what nobody tells you. Indonesians are some of the warmest
       people on earth. The second you try one phrase in Bahasa? They light up.
       Better recommendations. Local prices. The whole trip changes."

00:24–00:34  BRAND MENTION (story-embedded)
VISUAL: Phone screen — Speak Indo app, Bali survival deck visible.
LINE: "For round two I used Speak Indo for like five minutes a day for three weeks
       before my flight. They have a Bali survival deck — exact phrases for
       warungs, drivers, money, directions. It's free."

00:34–00:40  PAYOFF / SOFT CLOSE
VISUAL: Avatar — quick cut to a warung scene, ordering in Bahasa, locals nodding.
LINE: "Round two was a different country."
```

**Caption:** *Round two changed how I travel. What's the hardest "I should've learned the language" moment you've had?*
**Bio CTA:** Speak Indo — learn Bahasa in 5 min/day → speak-indo.com
**Pinned comment:** *the Bali deck is free, no card — link in bio*

## Alternate hooks

1. **Direct:** "If you've booked a Bali trip in the next month, save this."
2. **Comparison-bait:** "First Bali trip vs second Bali trip — and the only thing that changed was thirty phrases."
3. **Hot take:** "Hot take: Bali tourists who don't learn ten Indonesian phrases are leaving the best part of the trip on the table."

## Seedance / HeyGen production direction

- **HeyGen avatar:** Casual at-home or desk setup. Slightly upbeat but storyteller energy, not influencer energy.
- **Seedance B-roll prompts** (save to `prompts/`):
  - `"empty road in rural Bali Sidemen, rice terraces, scooter passing, tourist with backpack looking confused at handwritten sign"`
  - `"warung Bali, locals laughing with foreign customer, plate of nasi campur, golden hour, handheld documentary feel"`
  - `"hand counting Indonesian rupiah notes, market vendor in background smiling, shallow depth"`
  - `"scooter POV, Bali backroads, rice paddies, late afternoon light, no people"`
  - `"Canggu café, person speaking to barista, both smiling, natural light, Bahasa subtitle on screen"`

## Copy block velocity

- Pain ~25% (the Sidemen story — specific, cinematic)
- Promise ~25% (the "different country" payoff)
- Curiosity ~10% (the wedge: "what nobody tells you")
- Brand-as-mechanism ~15% (Speak Indo, 5 min/day, Bali deck)
- Native rhythm ~25%

**Believability rating:** 7/10 — leans on personal story (high believability) + plausible specifics (3 weeks, 5 min/day, exact deck name).
