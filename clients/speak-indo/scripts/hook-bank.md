# Hook Bank

Reusable hook patterns. Every script starts with one of these. The first 1.5
seconds is the whole game.

---

## Pattern 1 — "If you're going to Bali, ___"

The strongest hook for our audience. Names the destination + implies urgency.

- "If you're going to Bali, learn this before you land."
- "If you're going to Bali, save this Gojek phrase."
- "If you're moving to Bali, don't say *terima kasih* like this."
- "If you're flying to Bali next week, learn these 3 words."
- "If you're going to Bali, this is how you order coffee."

---

## Pattern 2 — "What to say in Bali when ___"

Names a specific situation. The audience instantly imagines themselves in it.

- "What to say in Bali when your Gojek driver calls."
- "What to say in Bali when you don't want spice."
- "What to say in Bali when you're checking into your villa."
- "What to say in Bali when locals speak too fast."
- "What to say in Bali when ordering nasi goreng."
- "What to say in Bali when asking the price."
- "What to say in Bali when meeting your partner's family."

---

## Pattern 3 — "Don't say ___, say ___"

Pattern interrupt. Implies the viewer is already doing it wrong.

- "Don't say *no spicy*. Say *jangan pedas.*"
- "Don't only say *terima kasih.* Say this instead."
- "Don't call your driver *sir*. Say *bli* or *bang*."
- "Don't say *cheap*. Say *boleh kurang?*"

---

## Pattern 4 — "X phrases that ___"

Numbered listicle. Promises a payoff.

- "3 Bahasa phrases that make locals smile."
- "5 words every Bali tourist needs."
- "4 Indonesian phrases for your first day in Bali."
- "3 phrases to survive a warung."

---

## Pattern 5 — Contrarian / curiosity

- "Most tourists in Bali only learn one word. Here's the second one you need."
- "Indonesians won't tell you this, but…"
- "This is the Bahasa phrase that gets you a local price."
- "Stop bowing in Bali. Do this instead."

---

## Pattern 6 — Journey / POV (foreign learner only)

- "Day 3 of learning Indonesian for Bali."
- "I tried ordering nasi goreng in Indonesian — here's what happened."
- "I asked the AI Bahasa coach how to talk to my driver."
- "5 minutes a day for 7 days. Here's what I can say now."

---

## CTA bank

Always *practice*, never *download*.

- "Practice this free at speak-indo.com."
- "Try saying it free at speak-indo.com."
- "Drill this with the AI coach — link in bio."
- "Save this and practice at speak-indo.com."
- "Take the free 7-day Bali Bahasa challenge — link in bio."

## Hook scoring (week 1 → week 4)

After each post, log in `metrics/north-star.md`:

- 3-second retention
- Profile clicks
- Link clicks
- Free signups

By end of week 1, identify the **top 3 hook patterns**. Week 2 onward, 70% of
new scripts should remix those three.
